Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is it normal to feel overwhelmed?

Yes. Everyone faces times when stress or sadness feels too heavy. Admitting you feel overwhelmed is the first step toward getting relief.

Q: Will anyone find out if I reach out for support?

All support requests submitted through our non-profit platform are kept strictly confidential by our team and counselors.
